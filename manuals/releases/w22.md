# W22 Release Manual

Features shipped in the W22 release across the WebSite and BackOffice. Each entry below links to the (release-agnostic) feature manual under **Manuals by Domain**.

The matching QA test plan is tracked separately as a Notion task: [Test pending features — Staging → Production (W22)](https://www.notion.so/36cbd1aae1c981cca636faa8a89b5227).

## SkyStories

- [Related Tours](../domains/sky-stories/related-tours.md) — Sky Story detail pages now end with a Related Tours rail, populated and rendered in every locale.
- [Captions & Media Editing](../domains/sky-stories/captions-and-media-editing.md) — captions and alt text on media tiles are now editable inline in BackOffice; the previous optimistic-save flicker is fixed.
- [Keyword Multi-Locale Sync](../domains/sky-stories/keyword-multi-locale-sync.md) — keywords created in the Taxonomy view now auto-publish to every locale and propagate to Strapi without manual per-locale entry.

## Bookings

- [Payment-Before-Confirmation](../domains/bookings/payment-before-confirmation.md) — **SSA-621** reworked the booking flow so customers pay first, then the booking is confirmed.
- [Multi-Tour Promos](../domains/bookings/multi-tour-promos.md) — **SSA-633** and **SSA-634** added multi-tour scope on promotions and a pax-stepper clamp at the promotion's `min_pax`.
- [Customer Notes — End-to-End](../domains/bookings/customer-notes.md) — **SSA-619** captures customer notes at checkout. **SSA-619 D5** gates the edit affordance in BackOffice so staff cannot silently overwrite customer-typed notes.
