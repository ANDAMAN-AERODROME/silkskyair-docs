W22 Release Manual

Features shipped in the W22 release across the WebSite and BackOffice. Each entry below links to the (release-agnostic) feature manual under Manuals by Domain.

The matching QA test plan is tracked separately as a Notion task: Test pending features — Staging → Production (W22).

SkyStories

  • Related Tours — Sky Story detail pages now end with a Related Tours rail, populated and rendered in every locale.
  • Captions & Media Editing — captions and alt text on media tiles are now editable inline in BackOffice; the previous optimistic-save flicker is fixed.
  • Keyword Multi-Locale Sync — keywords created in the Taxonomy view now auto-publish to every locale and propagate to Strapi without manual per-locale entry.

Bookings

  • Payment-Before-Confirmation — SSA-621 reworked the booking flow so customers pay first, then the booking is confirmed.
  • Multi-Tour Promos — SSA-633 and SSA-634 added multi-tour scope on promotions and a pax-stepper clamp at the promotion's min_pax.
  • Customer Notes — End-to-End — SSA-619 captures customer notes at checkout. SSA-619 D5 gates the edit affordance in BackOffice so staff cannot silently overwrite customer-typed notes.
