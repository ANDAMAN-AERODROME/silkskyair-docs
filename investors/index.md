# Investor Documentation

**The Andaman Aerodrome Network Platform (AANP)** — a turn-key platform for aviation
activities built on a network of helipads and heliports, with white-label commercial
services as its value-added layer, and **SilkSkyAir** as the first commercial operator
built on it.

This suite is written for investors focused on the **expansion, ownership and management
of the operational network** — heliports, helipads and aircraft — and on the platform's
capacity to host an unlimited number of commercial operators on that base.

Every technical claim and metric in these documents is derived from, and verifiable
against, the platform's source repositories.
